Privacy policy

Last updated: 29 October 2026.

Folio Player Plus is a video player. It does not run any server, has no account of its own, shows no ads and contains no analytics or tracking. The developer does not collect, store or share your personal data.

What stays on your device

  • Your library list, your settings and your viewing progress.
  • Passwords, server logins, API keys and sign-in tokens, kept in the Keychain on your device.
  • The date your free trial started, kept in the Keychain so that reinstalling the app does not restart it.
  • Your videos stay where they are. The app reads them to play them and does not upload them anywhere.

Services you choose to connect

The app talks to a service only when you set it up or use the feature that needs it. These services receive the information needed for that feature and have their own privacy policies.

  • TMDB (posters, summaries, cast): film and show titles, years and identifiers, with the free API key you create.
  • OMDb (IMDb and Rotten Tomatoes ratings): film identifiers or titles, with your free API key.
  • OpenSubtitles (subtitles): film titles or file identifiers, with your free API key.
  • Trakt (history, progress, calendar, recommendations): what you watch and follow, only if you sign in to your own Trakt account. You can disconnect in Settings.
  • Your own servers (Plex, Jellyfin, Emby, WebDAV, SMB, SFTP, FTP, DLNA) and video links: the app connects to the addresses you enter, with the logins you enter.
  • YouTube: the trailer button opens YouTube (the app or the website).
  • AirPlay: when you cast to a TV, the film is sent to the device you pick.

iCloud

If you turn on iCloud sync (a Plus feature), your library list, server entries and progress are stored in your own iCloud account and synced between your devices by Apple. Passwords go through iCloud Keychain. The developer cannot see this data.

Purchases

Plus is bought through the App Store. Apple handles the payment and does not give the developer your payment details.

Children

The app is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect any data from them.
