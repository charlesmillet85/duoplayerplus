Getting started

  • The app plays your own videos. Add files, folders, SMB shares or a video link with the plus button on Home.
  • Posters, ratings and subtitles use free keys that you create yourself: TMDB, OMDb and OpenSubtitles. A guided setup in the app walks you through each one, and you can skip any step. You can open it again in Settings › Sources.
  • Calendar, My journey and Discover need a personal Trakt account.

Free trial and Plus

  • Every new install has every feature unlocked for 14 days. After that the player and your own files, folders, SMB shares and links stay free.
  • Plus is a single purchase with no subscription. It unlocks Plex, Jellyfin, Emby, WebDAV, SFTP, FTP and DLNA servers, offline downloads, Trakt features and iCloud sync.
  • To restore a purchase, open Settings › Plus and choose Restore purchase, signed in with the Apple Account that bought it.

Playback

  • Supported files: MKV, MP4, M4V and MOV. HEVC and H.264 video, with HDR10, HLG and Dolby Vision as far as your screen allows.
  • DTS and TrueHD sound is converted on the fly.
  • Subtitles: SRT, ASS, WebVTT, PGS and VobSub, from the file or from OpenSubtitles.

Common questions

A film shows only its file name. Add a TMDB key in Settings › Accounts, then refresh the library. Names like "Title (2024) 1080p.mkv" or "Show S01E02.mkv" are recognised best.

A server does not connect. Check the address and that the server is reachable from your network. Plex, Jellyfin and Emby need a server of your own.

The picture has black bars. Choose Fill screen in the player.
